        ## Overview
        * Teachers: Fabien Siron and Corentin Vigourt
        * Teaching hours (total): 25h
        * Language: French
        * Institution: EPITA
        * Audience target: M1/M2 students in real-time and embedded specialization
        ## Abstract
        The course introduces software verification and validation techniques
        for complex and safety-critical software.

        It covers both dynamic and static approaches, including **functional and structural testing**,
        **static analysis**, **fuzzing**, and an introduction to **formal verification**
        and **model checking**.
        
        The course combines lectures with practical sessions in which students
        apply these techniques.
