  The paper studies the integration of safety-critical software on multicore
  platforms in the context of AMC 20-193. It proposes a time-aware approach
  based on the synchronous Logical Execution Time paradigm to separate
  functional interference concerns from timing interference concerns and to
  simplify reasoning about multicore integration and certification.
