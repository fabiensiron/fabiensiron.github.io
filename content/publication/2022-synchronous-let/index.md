abstract: >-
  This paper introduces the synchronous Logical Execution Time paradigm, which
  combines the explicit logical durations of LET with logical clocks and
  synchronization mechanisms inspired by Synchronous-Reactive languages. The
  resulting model aims to retain implementation-oriented timing information
  while enabling synchronous reasoning and verification techniques.
